How the try-on button gets there

  1. Open the model in iJewel Drive or iJewel Playground.
  2. Under the TryOn Settings tab, switch on Enable AR and fit the ring to the sizing cylinder.
  3. Save, then use Embed (</>) → Copy HTML code.
